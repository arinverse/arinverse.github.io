MeasureX

AR ruler and tape measure for Android

MeasureX measures real objects and spaces through your phone's camera, using Google ARCore — a doorway, a wall, a worktop, a room, a parcel. It measures distance, rectangles, circles, triangles, angles and volume, shows the full breakdown for each, and exports a project as a PDF report, an image sheet or a CAD (DXF) drawing.

  • Measuring, saving and exporting work fully offline
  • No account and no sign-in
  • Metric and imperial, including feet-and-inches
  • Requires an ARCore-supported device and camera permission
