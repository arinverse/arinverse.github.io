Privacy Policy — ScribbleBeat

ScribbleBeat is a 2D animation studio for Android, published by Arinverse.

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · Applies to the Android app com.arinverse.scribblebeat

The short version

ScribbleBeat has no accounts and no sign-in. Every drawing, animation, picture, sound and video you work with stays on your phone. We cannot see any of it, because the app never sends it anywhere. Drawing, animating and rendering all happen on your device.

ScribbleBeat shows no ads and runs no analytics or crash reporting. The app itself does not use the internet at all: its release build does not even hold Android's internet permission. If you buy ScribbleBeat Pro, Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details.

What the app does with your work

Your animations are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Pictures, videos and sound files you bring in are copied into the project they are used in, and processed entirely on your device — for example to trace over a video, or to time mouth shapes to a sound for lip sync. Nothing you create or import is uploaded, and no copy of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

When you render a video, GIF or pictures, the file is saved in your phone's shared storage (in a ScribbleBeat folder under Movies, Pictures or Downloads) or handed to Android's share sheet when you choose to share it. Backups and animation files you export are saved wherever you choose. Once saved or shared, those files are yours and are no longer managed by the app.

What the app stores on your device

  • Animations — your drawings, timelines, scenes and settings for each project, with the pictures and sounds you added to it.
  • Your Kit — drawings and Motion Units you saved to reuse in other projects.
  • Settings and progress — app preferences, which lessons you have finished, and similar app state.
  • Pro status — whether you have ScribbleBeat Pro and which plan, as last confirmed by Google Play, so Pro keeps working without a connection.
  • Render cache — temporary files from an interrupted render, which you can clear in Settings → Storage.

What the app sends and downloads

Nothing. ScribbleBeat makes no network requests of its own and downloads nothing. Two Google features talk to the Google Play Store app on your phone, which handles any connection itself:

  • Buying and restoring Pro, through Google Play Billing (see Purchases).
  • Rating the app, through Google Play's in-app review card, which may appear after you have finished a few renders. Your rating and review go to Google Play and are handled under Google's own Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Every drawing and animation tool in ScribbleBeat is free. ScribbleBeat Pro is an optional subscription, monthly or yearly, sold through Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your card number or payment details — Google processes those under its own Privacy Policy. The app only learns from Google Play whether you have an active purchase and which plan it is, and keeps that on your phone. It is never sent to us.

You can manage or cancel a subscription in the app (Settings → Manage subscription) or in Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

What we do not do

  • We do not operate a server that receives your animations, pictures, videos or sounds.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not show ads, and the app contains no advertising code.
  • We do not run analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.

Permissions the app requests

PermissionWhy it is needed
POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC, WAKE_LOCK So a long render can keep going, with a progress notification, if you switch to another app or the screen turns off while it works.
com.android.vending.BILLING, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Added by Google Play Billing, so Pro can be bought and restored through the Play Store app.

ScribbleBeat asks for no storage, photo, camera, microphone, location or contacts permission. When you import a picture, video, sound or backup, you choose the file in Android's own file picker, which gives the app access to that one file only. Saving renders to shared storage needs no permission on Android 10 and later.

Children

ScribbleBeat is a general-purpose creative tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because the app holds your data only on your own device, you already have direct control over all of it: delete an animation inside the app, clear the render cache in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored. Files you exported, shared or backed up are kept where you saved them until you delete them.

Depending on where you live — under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act in India, or state privacy laws in the United States — you may also have rights concerning any data Google holds about your purchase, which are best directed to Google directly, since they, not we, hold that data.
