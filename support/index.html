Before you write in

Zubaan

Offline AI subtitle studio

  • Transcription quality issues are usually fixed by installing the larger "Better accuracy" language pack from Settings → Languages.
  • A stuck import or export almost always means the phone ran low on storage mid-job — free up space and retry.
  • Subscription or billing questions: include the email or Google account you subscribed with.

MeasureX

AR ruler and tape measure

  • Poor tracking is usually a lighting or surface issue — try a well-lit, textured surface rather than a blank wall or glass.
  • MeasureX requires an ARCore-supported device; unsupported devices are listed by Google here.
